# Northline Audio — Aurora H7 No-Audio Support Bulletin, version 2026-09-02

Illustrative internal support bulletin for a fictional customer-service demo. Applies to SKU AH7-BLK, Aurora H7 wireless headphones.

## Symptom: right earcup has no audio

1. Confirm the headset is charged and the source device's left/right audio balance is centered.
2. Hold Power and Volume Down for 10 seconds to reset the headset, then pair it again.
3. In the Northline Audio app, update Aurora H7 firmware to version 2.4.1 or later, then test both channels with a second source device.

If audio is still absent after these steps, record the results and escalate to the support team for defect confirmation and an RMA decision. Customer statements that a reset was attempted do not establish that the balance, firmware, and second-device checks are complete. The agent may suggest these steps and offer escalation; it must not say a hardware defect is confirmed.

## Coordination with returns policy

This bulletin describes product troubleshooting only. The Northline Audio Returns Policy controls the 30-day standard window, the 45-day suspected-defect review window, shipping, fees, and refund timing. Do not infer policy terms from this bulletin.
